**HobbyHub helps community hobby-group coordinators manage members and group activities from the keyboard.** It keeps member details, hobbies, skill levels, availability, and group memberships in one local application. Its command-line interface makes it quick to search and update records, while the JavaFX graphical interface keeps information easy to browse.

Coordinators use HobbyHub to organize their community and record their own matching decisions. The application does not connect members directly or make automatic matching decisions.
